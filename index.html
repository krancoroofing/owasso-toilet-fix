Our Toilet Repair Services

We handle the full range of toilet problems. Running toilet repair is one of our most common calls. A toilet that runs constantly can waste hundreds of gallons of water per day and inflate your water bill, and the cause is usually a worn flapper, a faulty fill valve, or a flush valve seal that no longer holds. We test each component, replace only what has failed with quality parts, and set the water level correctly so the toilet fills quietly and stops completely. Leak repair covers water at the base, damp drywall behind the tank, and supply line drips. We distinguish between harmless condensation and a failing wax ring or cracked tank, because the fix is very different, and we reseat toilets with a fresh wax ring and secure closet bolts when the seal has failed. Clog removal ranges from a simple obstruction cleared with professional augering equipment to recurring blockages that point to a deeper issue in the drain line, which we will identify and explain honestly. We also repair and replace flush handles, chains, canister valves, and dual-flush mechanisms, stop rocking or unstable toilets by correcting flange and mounting problems, and provide complete toilet replacement when a fixture is cracked, inefficient, or simply beyond economical repair. Replacement includes removing the old unit, inspecting the flange, installing a modern water-efficient toilet, and hauling the old fixture away.

Common Toilet Problems We Fix

Knowing the signs helps you call before a small issue becomes water damage. A toilet that runs intermittently often has a slow flapper leak. Weak or incomplete flushing can mean blocked rim jets, a low tank level, or a partially obstructed trapway. Gurgling sounds point to venting or drain issues that deserve prompt attention. Water around the base almost always means the wax seal has failed and should be addressed quickly to protect the subfloor. A handle that must be held down, a tank that refills on its own, phantom flushing at night, and sweating tanks in humid weather are all problems we solve daily. We will always tell you whether a repair is worthwhile or whether replacement is the smarter long-term value, and we will show you the failed part so you can decide with confidence.
